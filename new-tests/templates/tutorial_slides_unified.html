Introduction

The power grid is a network of stations, lines, and loads. It must stay balanced at all times to keep electricity reliable.

Operators use data and visual interfaces to monitor and control the grid.

In this study you will use two interfaces:

  • List View
  • Map View

Your objective is to solve tasks in both interfaces.

When solving the tasks, keep these priorities (in order):

  1. Safest
  2. Cheapest
  3. Cleanest
